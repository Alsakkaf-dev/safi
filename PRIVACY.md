# Privacy policy

Safi does not collect, store or transmit personal data.

- **Searches and browsing.** The extension reads the links on search result pages (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo) to compare their domains with block lists. This comparison happens inside your browser. No query, URL or domain is sent to the developer or to any third party.
- **Network requests.** The only requests Safi makes are downloads of block-list text files from their public addresses (by default `raw.githubusercontent.com`), once a day and when you press "Update now". These requests carry no information about you beyond what any file download reveals to the host (such as your IP address).
- **Storage.** Your settings, your own block and allow lists, downloaded lists and a count of flagged results are kept in the browser's local extension storage on your device.
- **Reports.** Reporting a site opens a GitHub issue form in a new tab. Nothing is submitted unless you submit that form yourself, under your own GitHub account.
- **No analytics, no ads, no tracking.**

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Alsakkaf-dev/safi/issues
